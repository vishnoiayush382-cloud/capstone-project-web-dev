A Movement, Not Just a Brand

Origine 1847 was created with the vision of redefining indulgence. In a world where desserts are associated with guilt, sugar crashes, and unhealthy choices, we introduced a new standard — where taste meets wellness.

We use premium natural sweeteners like monk fruit and allulose to craft chocolates and desserts that deliver the same rich experience without the harmful effects of sugar.
