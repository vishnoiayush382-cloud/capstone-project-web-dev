What is Monk Fruit?

Small but mighty, the monk fruit is a traditional treasure from the misty mountains of Asia. Known as the "longevity fruit," it offers an intense, clean sweetness without the metabolic burden of sugar.

Why Origine 1847 Uses It

  • Zero glycemic impact, maintaining steady energy.
  • Exceptional purity with no bitter aftertaste.
  • Nature’s perfect companion for artisanal chocolate.
  • Crafted for clinical-grade health standards.
